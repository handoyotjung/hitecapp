📱 Mobile Field Guide

How to Use HitecApp on Your Mobile Phone

Welcome to HitecApp! This simple guide shows you how to take inspection photos, speak captions with your voice, and sync your work with your team.

✓ One-column fast mobile view
✓ Hold mic to speak captions
✓ Zero data loss auto-save
🌟 Newbie First Step: Set Up Your Project

Before Taking Photos, Choose or Create a Project!

The camera buttons stay locked until you have an active project. Follow one of the two options below:

Option A 2 Fast Taps

Pick an Existing Project

  1. Look at the Project dropdown box.
  2. Tap Select a project...
  3. Tap your facility or plant name (e.g. Cilacap RU IV Zone 1).
✅ Ready! Company, City, and all photos load instantly.
Option B New Plant / Survey

Create a Brand New Project

  1. Type Company (e.g. PT Safety Indonesia Utama).
  2. Type City (e.g. Jakarta).
  3. In New project name..., type your survey title.
  4. Tap the + Create button.
🚀 Unlocked! Camera buttons are now ready to use.

1

Log In & Session Security

Easy login on Chrome or Safari mobile browsers

1. Open your mobile browser and enter your Email and Password.

2. Tap the blue Log In button.

🔒 Zero Data Loss Protection

If you close your browser or walk away, your work is saved automatically. Just log back in and your photos and captions will be waiting for you!

2

Understanding the Mobile Single-Column

Designed for one-handed operation on plant floors

On mobile phones, everything is organized in one smooth vertical stream:

  • Top Bar: Shows AutoSave status and Collapse Details ▲ button.
  • Project Section: Pick plant name, company, and city.
  • Upload Buttons: Big buttons to take camera photos.
  • Photo Cards List: Your photos with voice mic and captions.
  • Bottom Bar: Save/Lock screen and Export buttons.
💡 Pro Tip: Tap Collapse Details ▲ to hide the company boxes. This gives you maximum screen space to see your photos!
3

Take Photos in the Field

Direct camera capture with automatic low-bandwidth compression

1. Tap the 📷 Take Photo (Camera) button.

2. Your phone camera opens. Snap your equipment, junction box, or hazard.

3. Tap Use Photo or OK.

4. The app automatically compresses the photo so it uploads instantly, even on slow 3G/4G plant networks!

📁 Gallery Option: You can also tap Choose Files to select multiple existing photos from your photo gallery.
4

Speak Your Captions (Hold to Talk)

Fast hands-free dictation right next to each photo

Next to every photo thumbnail, you will see a square [🎙️ Mic] button.

  1. Press and hold the Mic button with your thumb.
  2. The button turns RED (Rec).
  3. Speak your observation (e.g. "Ex d IIB T4 Junction Box with missing grounding strap").
  4. Release your thumb when done speaking.
  5. The app automatically writes your words into the caption!
🟢 Status Colors:
• Green text = Photo has a caption ✅
• Yellow text = Needs a caption ⚠️
5

Tap to Type or Edit Captions

Expandable 5-row text box for quick adjustments

If you prefer typing or want to edit what you spoke:

  1. Tap on the caption text below the filename.
  2. A large 5-row text box expands immediately.
  3. Type or correct your inspection notes.
  4. Tap the Green Checkmark ✅ on the bottom right to save.
  5. Tap the Red X ❌ on the top right if you want to clear the text.
6

Reorder Photos (Touch & Drag)

Change photo sequence for your final report

On the right side of each photo row, you will see a 6-dot grip handle (⠿).

  1. Touch and hold the 6-dot grip handle.
  2. Slide your finger up or down to the position you want.
  3. Lift your finger to drop the photo in place.
  4. The row numbers (1., 2., 3...) update automatically!
7

Lock Screen (View Mode) in the Field

Prevent accidental touches while walking the plant

While walking in hazardous areas, you can lock the screen so you don't accidentally delete or edit captions:

  • Tap the Save button on the bottom action bar.
  • The screen switches to View Mode (inputs are locked).
  • The button now says Edit.
  • Whenever you want to take more photos or edit notes, tap Edit to unlock!
8

Export Reports & Team Sync

Generate documents or sync live with team members

1. Instant Document Export:

On the bottom bar, select your photos and tap:

📄 Word (.docx) 📊 PowerPoint (.pptx) 📑 PDF Report

2. Multi-Device Real-Time Sync:

Up to 10 surveyors can work on the same plant project at the same time. Photos uploaded by your teammates appear on your screen automatically in real time!
