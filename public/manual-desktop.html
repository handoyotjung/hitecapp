First Step: Set Up Your Project

Choose or create a project before uploading photos

Important Notice: You must choose or create a project first before uploading photos or editing captions.
Option A: Pick an Existing Project
  1. Click the "Select a project..." dropdown.
  2. Click your plant / inspection name.
  3. Done! All photos uploaded by field surveyors load automatically.
Option B: Create a New Project
  1. Type Company name (e.g. PT Safety Indonesia Utama).
  2. Type City (e.g. Jakarta).
  3. Type your New project name.
  4. Click the + Create button.
  5. Done! Project is created and ready for batch uploads.
Step 1

Log In & Desktop Mode Selection

Open Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox. Select Desktop mode, enter your email and password, and click Sign In.

💡 Pro Tip: Selecting Desktop mode configures the wide 1440px 2-column studio layout for maximum efficiency on monitors.
Step 2

Desktop Two-Column Studio Overview

The desktop workspace divides into two panels: Left Column (35%) for photo queues, uploads, and reordering; and Right Column (65%) for the high-res photo carousel and caption/observation editor.

💡 Pro Tip: Use your keyboard Arrow Keys (Left / Right) to quickly flip through inspection photos in the Carousel Studio.
Step 3

Batch Upload Equipment Photos & Folders

Click Choose Files or Upload Folder in the left panel to upload whole site directories. Photos are automatically compressed on the fly for rapid cloud storage while keeping nameplates razor sharp.

💡 Pro Tip: You can upload up to 200 high-resolution equipment photos in a single batch without browser slowdown.
Step 4

Dual-Panel Workspace & Focus Navigation

Click any photo row in the left queue to open it in the right viewer. Status chips instantly show whether each photo has an approved caption (Green ✅) or needs assessor attention (Yellow ⚠️).

💡 Pro Tip: Hover over any thumbnail in the left queue to preview the filename, size, and quick caption summary.
Step 5

Visual Image Markup, Doodles & Color Selection

Highlight hazardous equipment zones, earthing studs, and enclosure gaps using engineering tools: Rect (Rectangle), Arrow, Doodle (Free Draw), or Text with various color options (Red for Ex d Enclosures, Amber for Earthing paths, Cyan for Cooling fins). Click Save Image to burn annotations directly into the report deliverables.

💡 Pro Tip: Use Red Rectangles to frame flameproof terminal boxes and Amber Arrows to verify electrostatic bonding continuity.
Step 6

Technical Captioning & Equipment Identification

Type detailed equipment specifications in the Caption field (e.g. TECO 3-Phase Induction Motor 2.2kW, 220/380V, 50Hz, IP55 - Zone 1 HAC Assessment & Earthing Inspection). Captions auto-sync to cloud storage immediately.

💡 Pro Tip: Always include equipment certification details (e.g. Ex d IIB T4, IP rating) in the caption for compliance audit readiness.
Step 7

ATEX Assessor Observations & Risk Priority Grading

Document specific ignition risks, earthing checks, and standard compliance notes in the dedicated textfields. Assign Risk Priority Grades (F1 Red = Immediate Hazard, F2 Orange, F3 Yellow, F4 Green) and toggle Status between Open and Close.

💡 Pro Tip: Reference relevant European and International standards (e.g. IEC 60079-14 Section 9, EN 1127-1) directly in your recommendations.
Step 8

Drag-and-Drop Photo Sequence Management

Click and drag the 6-dot grip handle (⠿) on any left queue row to rearrange the photo sequence. The queue renumbers automatically (1., 2., 3...) and applies immediately to exported reports.

💡 Pro Tip: Group photos by process area or equipment skid (e.g. Overview first, followed by Nameplate close-up and Terminal Box).
Step 9

Save & View-Only Inspection Lock

Click Save on the bottom action bar to transition the workspace into View Mode. All inputs lock to prevent unintended edits. Click Edit anytime to resume modifications.

💡 Pro Tip: Switch to View Mode before presenting inspection findings in client review meetings to ensure data safety.
Step 10

Multi-Format Report Generation (PDF, PPT, Word)

Click Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), or PDF on the bottom PublishBar to generate client-ready inspection reports in seconds.

💡 Pro Tip: Exported Word documents retain all formatting and typography rules (!font16) established by PT Safety Indonesia Utama.
