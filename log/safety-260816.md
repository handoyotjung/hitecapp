## 2. Technical Implementation & Architecture

### A. Client Survey Platform (`survey.html`)
* **Framework**: Vanilla HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Google Outfit typography, Firebase Firestore compat SDK.
* **Assessment Factor Breakdown (13 Items across 3 Parts)**:
  * **Part A: Mobile Phone Experience (6 Items)**:
    1. Mobile Login & Top Header Layout
    2. Choosing & Creating Projects on Mobile
    3. Taking Photos & Background Upload Speed
    4. Typing Captions & Voice Input (Speech-to-Text)
    5. Reordering Photos with Finger (Drag & Drop)
    6. Downloading Reports on Mobile (PDF / PPT)
  * **Part B: Desktop Experience (4 Items)**:
    7. Two-Side Screen Layout (List + Zoom Editor)
    8. Gemini AI Safety Recommendations & Grammar Correction
    9. Reordering Photos with Mouse (Drag Handle)
    10. Exporting Final Reports (PDF, PowerPoint & Word)
  * **Part C: Multi-Device Sync & Trust (3 Items)**:
    11. Real-Time Sync Between Multi-Devices
    12. Overall Inspection Safety & Data Loss Trust
    13. Feature Wishlist & Improvement Ideas
* **Data Layer**: Direct integration with Firestore collection `client_surveys`.

### B. Live Engineering Debug Dashboard & Admin Panel (`progress.html` & `admin.html`)
* **Real-time Synchronization**: Firestore `onSnapshot` query streaming the latest 15 survey submissions dynamically.
* **Admin Panel Tab 3 Integration**: Dedicated `#feedback-section` positioned at the top of Tab 3 ("AI Agent & Audit") with live KPI counters (`live-survey-total`, `live-survey-avg`, `live-survey-count`).
* **Assessor Issue Extraction**: Auto-extracts qualitative user suggestions and low-rating flags (score ≤ 3.0) for rapid triage.
* **1-Click AI Debug Report**: `copyDebugReport(idx)` generates a structured Markdown report formatted for instant handoff to Anti & Cody.
